## 3. Step-by-Step Live Demo Presentation Script

### Step 1: Show the Home & Branding (1 min)
1. Double-click `start-pitch-demo.bat` in `growech-solution/restaurant-pos`.
2. Browser opens `http://localhost:3000`.
3. Point out: *"Notice that this is Growech Solution's tailored hospitality platform. We customize the branding with your restaurant's logo, colors, and legal receipts."*

### Step 2: Show the POS Ordering Flow (3 mins)
1. Open the POS screen.
2. Select a table (e.g., Table 4).
3. Tap food categories (Starters, Main Courses, Drinks).
4. Add items with modifiers (e.g., "Medium Rare", "No Onions").
5. Show Split Bill and Discount features.
6. Emphasize: *"Notice how snappy it is — no cloud loading spinner, instantaneous response."*

### Step 3: Show the Kitchen Display (KDS) (2 mins)
1. Switch to the KDS tab.
2. Show the newly punched order appearing instantly for the chef with color-coded timers (New -> Preparing -> Ready).
3. Say: *"Your chefs don't need to juggle greasy paper tickets anymore. One tap marks food ready, notifying waiters immediately."*

### Step 4: Show Table & Floor Plan (2 mins)
1. Open the Table Floor Map.
2. Demonstrate how a hostess sees at a glance which tables are waiting for food, currently eating, or ready for bill settlement.

### Step 5: The "Unplugged Internet" Closing Proof (The Dealmaker)
1. Tell the owner: *"Watch this."* Turn off Wi-Fi or unplug the WAN cable.
2. Punch an order, create a bill, generate an invoice.
3. Everything continues working without interruption!
4. Closing line: *"When your competitor's cloud POS goes dark during Sunday dinner rush, Growech Dine keeps your kitchen running and payments flowing."*
